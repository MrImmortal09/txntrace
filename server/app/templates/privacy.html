TxnTrace Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

TxnTrace is a personal expense tracker. It reads the bank SMS you give it, turns them into transactions, and helps you track card dues, rewards and money split with friends. This policy covers the TxnTrace apps for iPhone and Android and the website at txn.axiosiiitl.dev.

In short: your data stays on your phone unless you sign in to Cloud Backup & Sync. If you do, it is stored on our server so you can back it up, restore it and use the website. We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

Who runs TxnTrace

TxnTrace is made and run by Om Swami. For any question about your data, or to ask for it to be deleted, email reach.oms2004@gmail.com.

Data kept on your phone

Without an account, everything below stays in the app's storage on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Messages. On iPhone, the messages your Shortcuts automation hands to the app, or that you paste in. On Android, the SMS your phone receives, which the app reads with your permission. The app keeps a log of them so it can show what it parsed and what it couldn't.
  • Transactions parsed from those messages or from statements you import: bank, amount, merchant, date, reference number, the last four digits of the account or card, balance, category and your notes.
  • Location of a transaction, only if you set up your Shortcuts automation to pass it. The app itself never asks for your location.
  • Cards you add: name, bank, last four digits, credit limit, statements and rewards.
  • Friends and splits. When you split an expense, you pick a contact from your phone. The app uses only the contact's name.

Bank statements you import in the app are read on the phone. Settings → Reset App deletes all of it from the phone, and so does removing the app.

Data stored on our server, if you sign in

Signing in is optional and only needed for Cloud Backup & Sync and the website. You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code. Once you do, the following is stored on our server and linked to your account:

WhatWhy
Your phone numberIt identifies your account, and the sign-in code is sent to it.
Your transactions, including the original message text and any locationBackup, restore, and showing them on the website.
Your cards, with their statement and due days, reward program, the rewards you recorded and the statement amounts you enteredSyncing cards between the website and the app, and backup and restore.
Names of the contacts on your phone, if you have given the app contacts access. Names only, never phone numbers or other details.So the website can split expenses with the same people.
Splits and settlements: who owes what, and payments receivedShowing the same balances on the website and the app.
Statements you upload on the websiteThe file is read to find transactions and is not kept. The transactions you choose to import are stored.

The log of all your messages is never uploaded; only the text of messages that became transactions is. The website uses one cookie to keep you signed in, and no tracking cookies. Like most web servers, ours keeps short-lived logs of requests, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

Your data is used only to provide these features. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold, rented or shared with anyone for their own use.

Services that handle your data for us

  • Oracle Cloud runs our server, and Cloudflare carries traffic to it. All traffic between the app or website and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Neon hosts the database where server data is stored.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) delivers the sign-in code to your phone number as a WhatsApp message, so WhatsApp handles your number and that message under its own privacy policy. The server that sends the code runs on Google Cloud.
  • Google Maps. When you tap a transaction's location, it opens in Google Maps, which receives that location under Google's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Google otherwise.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code.

Deleting your data

  • Your account and server data: in the app, go to Settings → Cloud Backup & Sync → Delete Account. This deletes your account and everything stored on our server for it at once, and can't be undone. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.
  • Data on your phone: Settings → Reset App, or remove the app.

Deleted data can stay in the database's automatic backups for a short time before they are overwritten. We don't restore anything from those backups once an account is deleted.

Your choices

  • You can use TxnTrace without an account. Nothing leaves your phone then.
  • You can turn off contacts access, or stop your Shortcuts automation, at any time in your phone's settings.
  • You can see your server data on the website, restore it to your phone from Settings → Cloud Backup & Sync, or ask us for a copy.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your data at the email address above.

Children

TxnTrace isn't meant for children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what is collected or how it is used, we will also say so in the app's release notes.
